Clinical Guideline Atlas

Cannabinoid Hyperemesis Syndrome

A comparative review of 11 institutional guidelines and clinical pathways for diagnosis and management of CHS.

Guidelines

Synthesized from 11 published CHS guidelines and clinical pathways (LaPoint 2018 – Hsu 2025) for research and educational reference. Not a substitute for institutional protocols or clinical judgment.

This resource is still under development and was funded by the University of Washington’s Cannabis Dedicated Account, developed by Isheeta Gupta, MPH, MSW, under the supervision of Sharon Garrett, MA, MPH, and Beatriz Carlini, PhD, MPH, from the Cannabis Education and Research Program (ADAI-CERP) at the University of Washington School of Medicine.
